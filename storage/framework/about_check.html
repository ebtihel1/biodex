About Biodex

Building the operating system for a circular economy

Biodex started from a simple observation: waste is only waste when the system around it fails. We connect the people who have it, the people who can process it, and the tools that make the exchange trustworthy.

  • One connected platform
  • Local AI services
  • Community driven
  • Transparent by design

Our mission

Turn environmental challenges into everyday opportunities

Biodex exists to make waste sorting, repair, recycling, and green consumption easier, more rewarding, and more accessible — for households, schools, associations, and businesses alike.

We believe sustainable change begins with simple actions, strong local communities, and transparent systems that connect people to better habits and measurable impact. Every donation, order, reservation, and campaign on the platform feeds the same loop: keep materials in circulation for longer.

People · planet · progress

What drives us

Three principles, one direction

Circular economy

We promote reuse, repair, and recycling to reduce waste and create sustainable value chains instead of one-way disposal.

Community engagement

We mobilize citizens, schools, associations, and businesses around shared environmental goals and visible local action.

Practical innovation

We combine machine learning with field knowledge to make green behaviours easier to adopt and simpler to measure.
